easyNote
    Notes are saved in this browser.

    Write or draw anywhere

    Shapes: draw a circle, rectangle, triangle or line and pause at the end of the stroke to snap it. Large shapes snap on their own.

    Smart Script: your handwriting is straightened and tidied after you pause.

    Math: switch to Math and write 12 × 3 =, a = 5 or y = x^2.

    100%

    Settings

    Claude recognition (optional)

    Recognition runs offline by default. If you add an Anthropic API key, math lines are also sent to Claude for more accurate reading. The key is stored only in this browser and sent only to the Anthropic API.
